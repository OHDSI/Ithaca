Task:
Produce a scoring rubric that determines whether the cohort label is
supported by NON-INDEX evidence in the record.

The rubric must output one of:
- YES
- NO
- INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE

CRITICAL DESIGN PRINCIPLES (must obey):

1) This is LABEL VALIDATION, not disease discovery.
   The question is:
   “Is the assigned phenotype label clinically plausible and internally
    consistent given the remaining claims evidence?”

2) Index condition diagnosis codes are NOT evidence.
   They are labels and have already been removed.
   Do not recreate them or rely on semantic inference of the diagnosis name.

3) Evidence types must be explicitly categorized.
   Prefer corroborating signals such as:
   - disease-specific procedures
   - disease-specific medications or supplies
   - peri-index management
   - post-index continuity of care

4) Single-signal validation is NOT allowed.
   A YES decision must require evidence from at least TWO DISTINCT
   evidence categories (e.g., procedure + medication).

5) Exclusion criteria MUST be explicitly modeled and evaluated BEFORE scoring.
   Exclusions are NOT negative evidence.

   There are two exclusion classes:
   A) HARD EXCLUSIONS:
      - Alternative explanations or situations that invalidate the label.
      - If triggered → decision must be NO or INSUFFICIENT.
   B) ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS:
      - Strong competing diagnoses or rule-out patterns.
      - These apply penalties and/or cap the maximum possible decision.

6) Evaluation order MUST be:
   (1) Hard exclusions
   (2) Alternative explanations
   (3) Positive evidence scoring
   (4) Decision thresholds

7) The rubric must be realistic for administrative claims
   and avoid reliance on unavailable data.
