<RUBRIC_RULES>

2) rules labeled "alternative_explanations" are rules that are used for differential diagnoses for a patient who may have <PHENOTYPE_NAME>
3) rules labeled "evidence_rules" are rules that are highly suggestive of <PHENOTYPE_NAME> and contribute to the diagnosis

Hard exclusions have a weight of -100 meaning that a subject who matches one of these rules is excluded from being a case.
Alternative explanations have weights between -1 and -5.  These rules don't preclude a patient from being a case but it requires the patient
to have more positive evidence (from evidence rules) to have a high enough score to be a case.

2) If the rule is questionable for <PHENOTYPE_NAME>, label it "Questionable"
